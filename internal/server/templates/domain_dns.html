How to enter these records

  • Most providers want the Name without the domain: for mail.{{$d.Name}} enter mail, and for the domain itself enter @.
  • Paste TXT values exactly, including the quotes a provider may add itself.
  • The PTR row cannot be set at your DNS provider: it lives in the reverse zone, which belongs to whoever gave you the IP. Open a ticket with them and ask for that name.
  • Two MX records with the same preference would be load-balanced; this plan uses one.
  • After propagating, press Verify all records — the status column then says what public DNS actually returns, not what you entered.
